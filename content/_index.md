I am a first-year PhD student and the Rupert Emerson Fellow in the [Department of Government](https://www.gov.harvard.edu/) at Harvard University. My research examines ethnic relations, elite ethnic appeals and media campaigns in South Asia. I am also interested in measuring how different religious groups express religious identity and how communal violence changes that expression.

I studied engineering (biotechnology) as an undergraduate. I worked as a data scientist for several years in the finance and technology industries. I subsequently completed a Master's in Development Practice at UC Berkeley and an ALM in Data Science at Harvard Extension School. More recently, I was a predoctoral fellow at Berkeley, where I worked with Professors [Edward Miguel](https://emiguel.econ.berkeley.edu/) and [Marshall Burke](https://web.stanford.edu/~mburke/) on using satellite imagery to measure the effects of a large cash transfer program in western Kenya.
